# VT – Printz 🖨️🎨

VT – Printz is a modern **printing and corporate branding service platform** that provides high-quality customized products and printing solutions for businesses, startups, and organizations.

Our goal is to help companies **build strong brand identity** through customized merchandise and professional printing services.

---

## 🚀 Services We Provide

VT – Printz offers a wide range of **corporate and printing services**, including:

### 👕 Corporate & Promotional Products
- Corporate T-Shirts with company logo
- Mobile stands with company logo
- Metal pens with company branding
- Custom office merchandise
- Promotional gift items

### 🖨️ Printing Services
- Visiting cards printing
- Pamphlets & flyers printing
- Brochures & marketing materials
- Corporate stationery
- Other custom printing solutions

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### 🌐 Frontend
- **React.js**
- Tailwind CSS (for responsive and modern UI)

### 🧩 Backend
- **Node.js**
- **Express.js**

### 🗄️ Database
- **MongoDB Atlas**

### ☁️ Cloud & Media Storage
- **Cloudinary** (for images and media Assets)
